# Grit and Growth Mindset

#### Question 1
**Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.**
Grit is the culmination of hard work, perseverance and the willingness to not give up easily for a long period of time in order to achieve a difficult dream or goal. It is like maintaining a constant rhythm in a marathon that piles up progress rather than a sprint. 

#### Question 2
**Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.**
A growth mindset serves as a base or the foundation for big goals. It depends mainly on putting in effort towards your goals, face challenges in order to be more confident, make mistakes to learn from them, take feedback as a positive sign.

#### Question 3
**What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?**
Internal Locus of Control means one believing in his/ her abilities, like hard work and perseverance, which can be controlled by the person to achieve his/ her goals.

Key Points:
* Having an internal locus of control is the key to staying motivated.
* Take time to solve problems in your own life and appreciate the effort that made it possible.

#### Question 4

**What are the key points mentioned by speaker to build growth mindset (explanation not needed).**
* Believe in your ability to figure things out.
* Question your own assumptions(specifically the negative ones).
* Follow a curriculum/ path that maps out your entire progress from start to end in achieving a goal.
* Honor the struggle. Try to maintain resilience when you fail or are knocked down.
