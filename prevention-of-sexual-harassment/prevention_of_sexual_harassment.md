# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

#### 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?
Sexual harassment can be caused in 3 ways: verbal, visual and physical.

##### Verbal harassment includes:
a.  Comments on clothes.
b. Comments on body.
c. Sexual or gender based jokes.
d. Spreading rumours about someone.
e. Using foul or obscene language.

##### Visual Harassment includes:
a. Obscene posters.
b. Drawings/ Pictures
c. Screensavers
d. Cartoons
e. Emails/ Text.

##### Physical Harassment includes:
a. Sexual Assault
b. Impeding or blocking movement.
c. Inappropriate touching.
d. Inappropriate gesturing.

#### 2. What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?
a. I would maintain a record of each incident, including the date, time, location, people involved, what happened, and any witnesses.
b. I would promptly bring the matter to the attention of the relevant manager, supervisor, or HR department so they can take appropriate action to address the behaviour and prevent it from happening again.
c. I would offer to listen to the person affected and their concerns without judging them, reassure them that the behaviour is not their fault, and encourage them to seek help if needed.
d. Offer to be a witness.
e. Report what was seen to local authorities.
