# Listening and Active Communication

#### 1. What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening? (Minimum 6 points).

* Avoid getting distracted by our own thoughts.
* Focus on the speaker and the topic.
* Avoid interrupting the speaker. Respond after they finish speaking.
* Show we are attentive through body language.
* Take notes during important conversations.
* Paraphrase and confirm with others to remain on the same page.

#### 2. According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening? (Write in your own words, use simple English)

* Listen more, talk less.
* Responding with honesty and acceptance rather than indifference and fake consideration.
* Trying to understand the meaning behind the conversation, not just facts and ideas.

#### 3. What are the obstacles in your listening process?

* Sometimes getting distracted by my own thoughts during listening.
* Interrupting the speaker, assuming they have finished speaking.

#### 4. What can you do to improve your listening?

* Focus more and try to think about what the speaker is trying to convey.
* Try not to interrupt the speaker actively.

#### 5. When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day to day life?

I usually use passive communication to communicate in my day-to-day life, but if I had to switch to it the situations could be asking for a favor or requesting something from someone.

#### 6. When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day to day life?

I don't usually communicate aggressively, but I might if I ask someone not to do something, assume a negative outcome, and they still do it and make the situation even worse.

#### 7. When do you switch into Passive Aggressive (sarcasm/gossiping/taunts/silent treatment and others) communication styles in your day to day life?

I usually use this type of communication with my friends and siblings during casual talks.
