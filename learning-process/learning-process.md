# Learning Process

### 1. What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.
Feynman Technique tells us to teach or explain a topic to others in simpler terms. If we cannot do it, we don't understand the concept ourselves.

### 2. In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

### 3. What are active and diffused modes of thinking?
* Active mode refers to a focused state where we can give all of our attention to a single problem or a task we have at hand.
* Diffused Mode refers to relaxed state where the mind wanders around. In this state, we think about random things throughout the day.

### 4. According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.
* Break down the topic into smaller chunks/ parts.
* Plan how the smaller topics actually let you reach your goal.
* Make sure to practice the smaller topics in a focused state.

## 5. What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?
* Use the Pomodoro technique to study.
* Break bigger topics into smaller chunks.
* Try to explain my understanding of the topic to others.
* Always start a task well before its given deadline.
