## Question 6
**Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do.**
According to the book, there are four laws of behaviour change that help us build good habits:

1. **Make it obvious** - This indicates that we must incorporate the things that remind us of the tasks we have to do into the environment.
2. **Make it attractive** - This tells us to pair a new habit with something we enjoy doing. This makes us feel motivated and crave the reward.
3. **Make it easy** - Reduce the things that divert you from starting a new habit, and start this in the environment you spend most time in. It also tells us to start small and build up.
4. **Make it satisfying** - We must reward ourselves for the smaller tasks and not just wait to accomplish a bigger goal.

## Question 7
**Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do.**
According to the book, we can make a habit harder to perform by reversing the four laws of behaviour change:

- **Make it Invisible:** Remove visual cues and reminders that trigger the unwanted habit.
- **Make it Unattractive:** Change how we perceive the habit so that it becomes less appealing.
- **Make it Difficult:** Introduce obstacles that make performing the habit more challenging.
- **Make it Unsatisfying:** Associate the habit with negative consequences or accountability so that we are less encouraged to repeat it.
